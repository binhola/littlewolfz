## Gravitational waves and cosmology
#### Properties of the late-time universe
- FLRW universe
$$
ds^2 = -dt^2 + a^2(t) d\mathbf{x}^2
$$
- Einstein equation:
$$
G_{\mu \nu} = \dfrac{8 \pi G}{c^4} T_{\mu \nu}
$$
- Friedmann equation
$$
H(z) = H_0 (\Omega_m(1+z)^3 + \Omega_\Lambda(1+z)^{3(1+w(z))})^{1/2}
$$

#### Dark energy
- Gravity is an attractive force, so we expect the expansion of the universe to decrease with time.
- But, we observed the contrary.
=> So we introduce new substance - dark energy
Properties:
- doesn't interact directly with us
- is diluted everywhere in the universe with const density $\rho \sim 10^{-28} \, kg \cdot m^3$ 
- not contribution of the Higgs boson to the vacuum energy $\rho \sim 10^{29}\, kg \cdot m^3$ 
=> Some tension here => motivate observation + theory
